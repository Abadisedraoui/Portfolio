Privacy Policy

This is Zainab Abadi's personal portfolio. Here is what happens to your information when you contact me or choose to accept analytics.

Last updated: 5 October 2026.

When you send me a message

The contact form sends your name, email address and message to Formspree, the service that delivers it to me. I use these details to respond to you from my email account.

Formspree stores form submissions by default. I use its Free plan, which provides 30 days of message history. The email delivered to me is a separate copy. Formspree may also collect technical details, such as your IP address, browser and the website you came from, to run the service and prevent abuse.

Formspree's servers are in the United States. Read Formspree's Privacy Policy for more about how it uses and keeps your information, and its security information for how it protects data, including international transfers.

You can send a message even if you decline analytics. If you prefer to contact me without using Formspree, you can email me directly at zainab.abadi.s@gmail.com.

If you accept analytics

Contentsquare helps me understand how people use this portfolio so I can improve it. If you select “Accept analytics”, it may collect the pages you visit, clicks and scrolling, your device type, operating system and browser, and your approximate city or country. This does not provide your home address.

The name, email address and message you type into the contact form are hidden in Contentsquare's session recordings. Technical details such as your IP address and a browser identifier may still be processed. These can count as personal data.

If you decline, Contentsquare does not load. You can still browse the portfolio and contact me. You can change your choice at any time using “Cookie settings” in the footer. Your choice is saved in your browser.

You can read more in Contentsquare's Privacy Center.

If you want a copy of a message you sent me, or would like me to delete it, email me at zainab.abadi.s@gmail.com.

Using the content on this site

The work, text and images on this site are shared to show my experience and design process. Unless stated otherwise, please ask permission before copying, sharing or reusing them.
