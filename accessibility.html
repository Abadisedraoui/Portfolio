Accessibility

I’m working to make this portfolio easier to use, including with a keyboard or assistive technology. WCAG 2.1 Level AA is the target for these improvements.

Last reviewed: 6 October 2026.

What has improved

Keyboard users can skip to the main content and see which control has focus. The contact form, card viewer and image galleries support keyboard controls. The contact form has labelled fields, explains that all fields are required, and keeps its confirmation open until you close it.

Text and form borders have been adjusted for contrast while keeping the portfolio’s visual style. Images have alternative text, and videos without speech have descriptions available to screen readers.

If you use your device’s reduced-motion setting, programmed scrolling is immediate and the cookie confirmation heart appears without its animation.

What has been checked

Desktop keyboard navigation has been checked in Chrome. The review also covered page headings, form labels, accessible names and representative contrast samples. These checks do not establish full WCAG compliance.

What still needs checking

Testing on real mobile screens, at high browser zoom, and with NVDA or VoiceOver is still pending. The reduced-motion setting also needs testing on a real device. Downloadable CVs need a fuller check of their accessible structure and reading order. Text alternatives for detailed charts and tables in the case studies also need a fuller review.

If something is difficult to use

Email me at zainab.abadi.s@gmail.com with the page and what you were trying to do. If helpful, include your browser or assistive technology. I can use that information to investigate the problem or share the content in another format where possible.
